How to read this

  • Both bodies play from the same clip time. The MHR reference is the motion source; the Mixamo body is the retargeted result.
  • Use Pin travel to cancel horizontal root motion so the bodies stay side by side. Turn it off to inspect the clip’s real travel.
  • Frame-step around a bad pose. Switch to Left or Right to judge knee, elbow and foot direction.
  • New conversions transfer captured finger and wrist motion. The project panel identifies older clips that still use a static hand pose.
  • Foot placement follows the source ankle movement within the avatar’s leg reach. Source tracking jumps and camera drift remain visible on both bodies.
