What is being tested

Stable foot silhouettes provide a prior for approximately stable depth. The revision adjusts placement to reduce disagreement with those image observations while preserving the original joint motion.

This first experiment uses reviewed foot regions in the opening eight seconds of this fixed-camera video. Silhouettes cannot prove unchanged depth. Overlapping feet, mask errors and motion along the camera ray remain ambiguous. This is an experimental candidate, not a published lesson update.
