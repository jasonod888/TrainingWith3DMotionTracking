How to assess this

Scrub through the bend and compare the real feet, their green masks and the depth graph. If the visible foot boundaries remain stable while estimated depth changes substantially, that supports false reconstructed travel. Inspect the enlarged view: a stable trouser edge is not necessarily a stable foot.

This experiment does not change your lesson or animation. Whole-person masks do not separate overlapping feet, prove contact, compensate for camera motion or recover floor geometry. The initial regions are review aids for this clip, not an automatic solution for other uploads.
