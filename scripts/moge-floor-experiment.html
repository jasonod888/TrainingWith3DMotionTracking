What is being tested

MoGe-2 runs on the first frame only. A fixed floor plane is fitted outside the person mask. Subsequent probable support-foot pixels are mapped to that plane to estimate travel; body placement changes while joint motion is preserved.

Experimental assumptions: fixed camera/lens, approximately flat floor, reviewed initial floor patch and foot search seeds. Tracking revision 2 uses foreground image-feature motion, adaptive mask searches and confidence with hysteresis. Support detection remains heuristic; raised or occluded feet and pose errors can still cause mistakes. Metric depth is predicted, not measured. The earlier silhouette method remains available in the selector. No published lesson is changed.
